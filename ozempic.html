Can you buy Ozempic in New Zealand?

No. Ozempic is not currently marketed in New Zealand, so pharmacies here have no stock to dispense and no retail price to quote. Searching for it on a New Zealand pharmacy site will usually redirect you to Wegovy.

Is Ozempic the same as Wegovy?

Both are semaglutide, made by Novo Nordisk. Ozempic is the brand licensed for type 2 diabetes and Wegovy the brand licensed for weight management, at a higher maximum dose of 2.4mg against Ozempic's 1.0mg. For weight management in New Zealand, Wegovy is the product that exists.

What about the prices I see quoted online?

Figures in the NZ$130–350 range circulating in articles are almost always Australian, where Ozempic is sold and partly subsidised. They don't apply here.

What is funded for type 2 diabetes instead?

Pharmac funds dulaglutide (Trulicity) and liraglutide (Victoza) under Special Authority criteria, dispensed at the standard prescription charge rather than a private price. Access was widened from 1 September 2026. Your GP applies; there is nothing to buy privately.

So what can I actually get for weight management?

Wegovy (semaglutide) and Mounjaro (tirzepatide) are both Medsafe-approved for weight management and available on private prescription, unfunded. Saxenda (liraglutide) is the older daily injection. Current prices for all three are on the front page.
