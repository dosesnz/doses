What NZ pharmacies charge for GLP-1 pens, checked every morning.

Wegovy, Mounjaro and Saxenda are not funded by Pharmac, so the price is whatever the pharmacy sets. This page reads the public price on each retailer's site once a day and puts them side by side. Pen prices only — a prescription and, for some pens, needles are extra. Use the calculator below for the full monthly figure.

What a month actually costs

Pen price at the cheapest listed pharmacy, plus whatever you pay for the prescription and needles. Change the numbers to match your own prescriber.

Wegovy pens include needles. Mounjaro KwikPens don't — a box of 100 is about $20–25, so roughly $1 a month at one needle a week.

Not on this page

Unichem and Life Pharmacy stores, Zoom Pharmacy (for Wegovy and Mounjaro) and Pharmacy Direct quote prices on request rather than listing them, so they can't be tracked here. Ozempic is approved in NZ for type 2 diabetes only and isn't sold for weight management. Funded GLP-1s for diabetes (Trulicity, Victoza) are covered by Pharmac under Special Authority and cost the standard prescription co-payment.

doses.nz is an independent price tracker. Prices are read automatically from each retailer's public website and may lag or differ from what you're charged in store — the retailer's site is the source of truth. Links to retailers may earn a small commission; it doesn't change the order or the numbers shown. Nothing here is medical advice: whether any of these medicines is right for you is a question for your doctor or pharmacist. Data sheets and consumer medicine information are on Medsafe.
